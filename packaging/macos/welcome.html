Metrobits (built on Micropolis)

Metrobits is Micropolis, the city simulator first released in 1989, rebuilt to run on a modern Mac with its original look and feel.

This installer puts Metrobits in your Applications folder. Your saved cities and settings will live in your Library, under Application Support, in a folder called Metrobits.

Metrobits is a modified version of Micropolis. Electronic Arts and Micropolis GmbH don't make, endorse or support it.
