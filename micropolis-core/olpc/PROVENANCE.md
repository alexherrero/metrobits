# Original game files

These files come from `micropolis-activity`, Electronic Arts' 2008 open-source release of Micropolis. They're copied unchanged from commit `c98f6b0` of [SimHacker/micropolis](https://github.com/SimHacker/micropolis/tree/c98f6b08519887b450d9be198bfca5237aab6d0c/micropolis-activity). The game reads them as they are: its pictures, a few of its sounds, and its font.

## Licence

`README.micropolis-activity` comes with these files. It puts the pictures and sounds under the GNU General Public License, version 3, with Electronic Arts' additional terms (`../MicropolisGPLLicenseNotice.md`). The DejaVu LGC fonts have their own licence, in `res/dejavu-lgc/LICENSE`.
